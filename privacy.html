Facetok Privacy Policy

Lucky Meadows™ · Last updated November 2026

1. Age requirement

You must be 18 or older to use Facetok. Every signup includes a verification video, reviewed by a human before access is granted. No automated age estimation, no exceptions.

2. Manual approval

Every account is personally reviewed and approved by the owner, Stephen Longino Wallace, before it can post. Pending accounts cannot see or touch the feed.

3. What we collect

Your email, username, verification video, posts, likes, and comments. Nothing else.

4. What we never do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Period. Your information stays within Lucky Meadows™.

5. Rejected or deleted accounts

If your signup is rejected, your verification video and signup data are deleted within 30 days. If you leave Facetok, contact the owner and everything tied to your account — posts, videos, profile — is deleted within 30 days.

6. Where your data lives

Posts and profiles live on our secure database (Supabase). Your login stays on your own phone. We log only what's needed to keep the app running.

7. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: the owner, through the app.
